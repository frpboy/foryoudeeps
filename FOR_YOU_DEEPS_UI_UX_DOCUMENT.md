## 1. Design Intent

This document defines how For You, Deeps should look, feel, move, and
behave from a human-centered UI/UX perspective. The site must feel like
a birthday gift someone carefully assembled over time. It must not feel
like a corporate employee appreciation page. It must not feel like a
developer portfolio. It must not feel like an ERP product landing page.
It must not feel like a generic birthday template. The interface should
be visually feminine without becoming childish. The emotional arc should
move from curiosity to delight, then nostalgia, connection, intimacy,
and a quiet final wish.

## 2. Core UX Philosophy

-   People first, interface second.
-   Authentic media over decorative filler.
-   Personal memories over corporate achievements.
-   Mobile-first over desktop-first.
-   Simple navigation over complex menus.
-   Natural scrolling over forced step-by-step navigation.
-   Small moments over constant animation.
-   Dark matcha as the emotional base.
-   Red as a controlled emotional accent.
-   Warm cream as the reading surface.
-   Playfulness without visual chaos.
-   Emotion without manipulation.
-   Polish without overengineering. \## 3. Emotional Journey

-   Vertical swipe for page movement.
-   Do not use pinch-to-zoom as a required interaction.
-   Do not require double-tap.
-   Do not rely on long press.
-   Do not make the whole page horizontally draggable. \## 39. Empty and
    Incomplete States
-   Gallery with no media: hide the gallery section rather than showing
    an empty gallery.
-   Wishes with no content: hide the wishes section until at least one

-   Journey with no content: omit the journey section.

    final greeting. The design should feel like a beautiful digital
    birthday scrapbook rather than a corporate microsite. Dark matcha
    should establish the identity. Red should provide emotional accents.
    Warm cream should provide readable surfaces. Level 31 Unlocked
    should provide the playful birthday motif. The gallery should hold
    memories. The wishes wall should hold people. The daughter's video
    should hold the most intimate moment. The final page should hold the
    simple birthday wish.

**Design principle to preserve throughout implementation:** the website
should never feel like it is showing off the developer; it should feel
like it is celebrating Deeps.
